Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Thank you for using Language Translator Pro. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Language Translator Pro does not require users to create an account.

The app may access the following features only when you use them:

  • 🎤 Microphone (for voice translation)
  • 🌐 Internet connection (to translate text using online translation services)
  • 💾 Local storage (to save translation history, favorites, and settings on your device)

Microphone Permission

The microphone is used only when you choose voice input. Audio is processed solely to convert your speech into text for translation. We do not permanently store or record your voice.

Internet Access

Internet access is required to perform translations using online translation services. Without an internet connection, translation features may not function.

Translation Data

The text you enter or speak may be sent securely to a translation service in order to provide translations. We do not sell or intentionally share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

History and Favorites

Your translation history, favorite translations, and app settings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to delete them.

Advertisements

The free version of the app may display advertisements. Advertising providers may collect limited technical information as described in their own privacy policies.

Premium Features

If you purchase premium features in the future, payment processing will be handled by the appropriate app store provider (such as Google Play). We do not collect or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Language Translator Pro is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your translation history.
  • Clear your favorites.
  • Uninstall the application at any time.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
